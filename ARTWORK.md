# Фоновые иллюстрации версии 1.1

Созданы встроенным ImageGen. Оптимизированные WebP-файлы включены в приложение и архив исходников:

- `dist/assets/forest-v2.webp` — библиотека, ознакомительная страница и глава I.

Промпты использовали следующую общую часть, после которой добавлялось описание конкретной сцены.

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide 16:9 background art for an immersive Russian winter horror novella reader. Premium hand-drawn graphite, charcoal and scratched ink illustration with intricate realistic environmental detail, restrained cinematic grayscale, almost black shadows, desaturated silver snow, soft film grain. Psychological folk horror atmosphere reminiscent of old illustrated winter ghost stories. No lettering, no typography, no logos, no characters, no monsters, no UI. Make it beautifully drawn and genuinely ominous, with convincing perspective, layered mist and subtle light. Not low-poly, not geometric vector art, not cartoon clipart. Leave center relatively calm and dark for readable text overlay.
